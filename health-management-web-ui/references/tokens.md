# Token direction

Accessible starting palette for an adult, supportive health product.

- Canvas `#F3F8F7`; surface `#FFFFFF`; subtle surface `#F7FAF9`; border `#D4E2DF`; text `#172522`; secondary `#52645F`.
- Brand teal `#0F766E`; hover `#115E59`; active `#134E4A`; soft brand `#E6F5F2`; focus `#0F6B63`.
- Secondary blue `#2563A6`; positive `#19733B`; attention `#9A5B00`; danger `#B42318`; unknown `#5F6B75`. Brand teal never substitutes for a measured “normal” state.
- Trend colors must remain stable and accessible, with direct labels or non-color encoding.

Use a 4px base, 8–12px radii, subtle elevation, 16px default body, and 44px primary targets when feasible. Favor comfortable reading over dashboard density. Use a Simplified-Chinese system sans stack; illustration must be original/licensed and optional, not structural.

Motion can support progress and continuity but must not pressure adherence. Avoid streak-loss animation and honor reduced motion.
