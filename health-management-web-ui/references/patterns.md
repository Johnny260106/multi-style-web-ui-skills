# Health-management patterns

- Today view: a small achievable plan, measurement status, relevant context, and the next useful action—not a wall of scores.
- Trend: metric name, unit, source, time range, baseline/target origin, missing data, meaningful annotation, and plain-language summary.
- Daily check-in: minimal burden, save-and-resume, “not measured” distinct from zero, optional context, and editable history.
- Plan: purpose, tasks, frequency, flexibility, responsible professional/source, pause/change path, and progress explained without blame.
- Reminder: user-controlled channel/time/frequency, privacy-aware preview, snooze/pause, and timezone behavior.
- Connected device: source, last sync, battery/connectivity where relevant, data confidence, manual-entry distinction, and reconnect help.
- Escalation: when supplied thresholds or user-reported symptoms require care, show the appropriate professional contact path without diagnosing.

Explain composite scores: inputs, time window, limitations, change drivers, and next step. Prefer interpretable measures when the score cannot be responsibly explained.
