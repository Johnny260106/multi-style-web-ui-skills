# States, boundaries, and anti-patterns

Cover no baseline, insufficient data, missed day, not measured, device disconnected, sync delayed, manual value, low confidence, plan paused, reminder snoozed, abnormal observation, professional review pending, and network failure. Never treat missing data as zero or failure.

Keep coaching separate from medical advice. Do not generate diagnoses, treatment changes, medication instructions, or urgent thresholds. Mark demo content and surface professional-review needs.

Avoid:

- opaque health scores as the dominant truth;
- streaks, leaderboards, guilt, or rewards tied to medical adherence;
- declaring a user “healthy” from incomplete consumer-device data;
- green/red-only trends and smoothed gaps;
- fake precision, unexplained targets, or fabricated AI insights;
- excessive wellness blobs, gradients, pills, and cheerful illustration during concerning states;
- notification text that exposes sensitive health information;
- claiming clinical, privacy, legal, or WCAG compliance without review.
