# Visual research: consumer health management

For a new product or substantial redesign, collect 12–20 current references across at least three sources, including two of Pinterest, Dribbble, Behance, and 花瓣; use 4–6 for a bounded component. Include a credible behavior-change, accessibility, or healthcare design reference alongside portfolio inspiration.

Search by behavior and evidence: `chronic condition tracking web`, `health coaching dashboard`, `sleep activity trends`, `nutrition plan web app`, `慢病管理 C端 Web`, `健康管理 数据趋势`, `运动饮食 计划 UI`. Prefer work from the last 18 months.

Log URL, date if known, journey, transferable pattern, behavioral or medical risk, and relevance. Compare daily burden, trend comprehension, missing data, source confidence, reminders, plan adaptation, escalation, and adult tone.

Synthesize across sources. Do not copy a complete composition, illustration system, brand identity, proprietary asset, or health claim. Validate visual novelty against comprehension and non-coercive behavior design.

Reject AI-default signals: abstract wellness blobs, generic green gradients, opaque health scores, streak pressure, trophy gamification for medical adherence, interchangeable metric cards, fake wearables data, excessive rounded pills, moralizing copy, and unqualified AI recommendations.

If a source is blocked, use indexed previews or another source without bypassing access controls. If browsing is unavailable or forbidden, disclose that the direction was not current-reference validated.
