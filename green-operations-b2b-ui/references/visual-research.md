# Visual research: green operations

For substantial work, collect 12–20 current references across at least three sources, including two of Pinterest, Dribbble, Behance, and 花瓣; use 4–6 for a bounded component. Prefer the last 18 months and compare portfolio concepts with a mature production system.

Search by operational problem: `renewable energy operations dashboard`, `carbon accounting platform`, `facility resource monitoring`, `agritech web app`, `能源管理 B端`, `碳管理 数据平台`, `园区运营 UI`. Do not search only for “green dashboard.”

Record URL, date if known, surface, transferable pattern, risk, and relevance. Compare target/baseline presentation, resource flows, time-series annotation, facility hierarchy, units, freshness, reports, and drill-down. Note whether green is being used as brand, success, or data encoding.

Synthesize multiple sources without copying composition, illustration, proprietary assets, or identity. Validate against data meaning, accessibility, and real actions.

Remove AI-default signals: leaf decoration unrelated to the product, green everywhere, generic eco gradients, oversized sustainability scores, identical KPI cards, fake charts, excessive pills, soft cards without hierarchy, and unqualified “AI insights.”

When a source is blocked, use indexed previews or another source without bypassing access controls. If browsing is unavailable or forbidden, disclose the limitation.
