# Green operations patterns

- Goal summary: actual, target, variance, period, baseline, owner, and status explanation—not a context-free score.
- Resource flow: source, transformation, destination, loss, unit, and time window. Provide a table alternative to Sankey or topology diagrams.
- Trend: label granularity, missing/estimated points, comparison basis, significant events, and freshness.
- Facility hierarchy: portfolio → site → zone/system → meter or asset, with consistent roll-up definitions.
- Efficiency comparison: normalize fairly, expose denominator, and explain incomparable entities instead of ranking them.
- Sustainability report: trace metrics to source/method/version and separate measured, calculated, and estimated values.
- Action plan: recommendation, expected impact, cost/effort, owner, due date, evidence, and status; do not present AI suggestions as facts.

On narrow screens, prioritize current status, exceptions, targets, and assigned actions. Move dense comparisons into focused detail views.
