# States and anti-patterns

Represent live, stale, missing, estimated, revised, pending verification, target changed, target met, target missed, and not applicable explicitly. Preserve source and calculation version when values change.

Exports and reports need preparing, ready, partial, expired, failed, and retry states. Long calculations need progress or meaningful status, cancellation when feasible, and background completion notification.

Avoid:

- green meaning both brand and “healthy” without labels;
- context-free carbon or efficiency scores;
- rankings with incompatible denominators;
- smooth lines over missing observations;
- decorative eco imagery replacing operational evidence;
- charts without units, time windows, baselines, or accessible summaries;
- claims such as “optimized” without a defined comparison;
- hiding estimated or revised data in footnotes.
