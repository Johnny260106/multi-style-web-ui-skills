# Visual research: industrial operations

Before a new product or redesign, collect 12–20 current references across at least three sources, including two of Pinterest, Dribbble, Behance, and 花瓣; use 4–6 for a bounded component. Prefer the last 18 months and compare showcase work with a production industrial or enterprise design system.

Useful searches combine workflow and environment: `manufacturing operations dashboard`, `equipment maintenance UI`, `warehouse exception console`, `industrial IoT web app`, `设备运维 B端`, `工单管理 后台`, `智慧工厂 UI`. Do not search only for “orange dashboard.”

For each reference record URL, date if known, operational surface, transferable pattern, risk, and relevance. Compare asset hierarchy, alarm treatment, work-order progression, telemetry density, units, timelines, maps, and operator actions. Prefer patterns that remain legible under pressure.

Synthesize multiple sources. Do not copy a composition, proprietary asset, icon family, brand identity, or dashboard screenshot. Treat portfolio work as visual evidence, not proof of safety or usability.

Reject AI-default motifs: identical KPI cards, orange applied to every status, gratuitous gradients or glow, decorative gauges, unlabeled charts, fake device IDs, excessive rounded panels, and control-room aesthetics on routine CRUD pages.

If a platform is blocked, use indexed previews or another source without bypassing access controls. If browsing is unavailable or forbidden, disclose that current visual research was not completed.
